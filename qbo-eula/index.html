3. What the App does with QuickBooks data

  • The App has read-only access: it reads invoices, payments, customers and standard financial reports (such as receivables aging and profit and loss) for internal reporting and planning.
  • The App does not create, change, send, or delete anything in QuickBooks. It does not process payments or payroll.
  • Data is stored only on MAKA Engineering's own computer in Manitoba, Canada, and is not shared with or sold to third parties. See the MAKA Power privacy policy.

4. Disconnecting

An authorized MAKA administrator can disconnect the App at any time, from QuickBooks (Settings → Apps) or from the App's disconnect page. Disconnecting revokes access and deletes the stored access tokens.
